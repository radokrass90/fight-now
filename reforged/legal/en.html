Privacy notice

Controller and contact

The controller is Anita Kardos (AKRKWebs). The postal address and contact details appear in the legal notice. You can send privacy requests by email or post.

Page requests and security

When you request the game page, its files or the online API, the servers involved receive technical connection data such as your IP address, the time and the requested resource. We process these data to deliver and secure the service. The API uses IP addresses for temporary rate limits on guest creation and sign-in. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interests are reliable service operation and preventing abuse.

Online profiles, access and progress

When you start an online profile, we create a player ID and session. You can choose a player name and password to use an account across devices. The server stores passwords as salted hashes. Please choose a player name that does not contain private information.

We store game currency, ledger entries, unlocked items, equipment, upgrades and online match data and outcomes so you can use and restore your progress. The legal basis for online features you request is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For technical abuse prevention we rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Online profile data are optional. Local play is available without them, while online saving and server rewards require a profile.

Browser storage

The game uses local browser storage for language, sound, music, effects, difficulty and your last fighter choice. With an active online profile, the browser also stores your session key, a profile copy and pending reward requests. Storage required for requested features is used under section 25(2)(2) TDDDG. You can clear it in your browser’s site settings. This may remove preferences and access to an unregistered guest profile. Clearing browser storage does not erase your server profile.

Hosting and recipients

Depending on the address you visit, the game page is served by GitHub Pages or the Fight Now game server. The game server provides the online API and progress database. When you use GitHub Pages, GitHub processes technical access data and may process them outside the EEA. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for recipient and transfer information.

Retention

A session is valid until sign-out or for up to 180 days from issuance. Session expiry does not delete the profile. Profiles and progress are kept to provide the online profile. If you request erasure, we assess which data must be erased and whether a legal obligation or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims requires further retention. Technical access data and rate limits are retained for service delivery and security according to those purposes.

Your rights

Subject to the GDPR, you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests under Article 21 GDPR. You can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, such as the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision. Use the contact details in the legal notice. Please do not send passwords or session keys.

Game terms

The game and online features

Fight Now is a browser fighting game depicting violence, weapons and finishing moves. There are currently no real-money payments. You need a compatible browser to play. Online profiles, saved progress and server rewards require an internet connection.

Virtual currency and items

Game coins are awarded in play. You can spend them on in-game items and upgrades. Coins and items belong to their profile and cannot be redeemed for money or transferred between profiles. Switching profiles does not merge progress.

Fair use

You may play the game for personal use. Do not use someone else’s credentials, tamper with progress requests or disrupt the service. Third-party content remains subject to its respective owners’ rights.

Contact and statutory rights

Use the contact details in the legal notice to report errors or ask questions. Mandatory consumer rights and statutory remedies for defects apply.

# Reforged visual art review — 2026-10-29

All fifteen normalized 30-pose contact sheets were inspected: Murat, Yasmin, Dragan, Kevin, Leyla, Tomasz, Hakan, Nadine, Emre, Boris, Jasmin, Adem, Marco, Fatima and Danny (450 poses).

Checked consistent costume/hair/identity, complete silhouettes, genuine transparency, guard and strike direction, low walking shuffle, raised uppercut, kick chamber/recovery, crouch/body-hit, fall/ground/rise. Dedicated Murat and Danny correction poses are present. No figure touches an outside source-sheet edge. Fixed sprite pivots and supporting-foot baselines are audited separately.

Contact sheets show extracted source scale; the runtime and interactive gallery apply a shared standing-height calibration per source strip. This compensates for wider ground poses constraining the packed strip scale without normalizing each animation frame independently. Air and floor poses retain their intended height changes.

These are painted frame-based 2.5D animations, not skeletal 3D characters. The browser test and actual atlas renderer check are recorded separately; physical iOS/Android devices have not been tested.
